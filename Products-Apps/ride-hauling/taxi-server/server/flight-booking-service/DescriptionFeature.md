# Flight Booking Service

## The Idea
The Flight Booking Service expands the Queens ecosystem into multi-modal transport by allowing users to search, reserve, and pay for commercial flights. This transforms the platform from a local mobility app into a comprehensive travel companion.

## Business Model Contribution
- **Commission & Affiliate Revenue:** Earns a percentage fee on every flight ticket sold through integrated airline APIs (e.g., Amadeus, Sabre).
- **Ecosystem Lock-in:** By handling flight bookings, the platform can automatically upsell airport transfer rides to the user, capturing both ends of the travel journey.

## Core Features
- **Booking Orchestration:** Manages the multi-step transaction process of reserving a seat and confirming ticketing.
- **Payment Processing Integration:** Handles high-value transactions securely, integrating with 3rd-party payment gateways.
- **Cross-Selling Engine:** Triggers the Ride Service to offer automated airport drop-off/pickup dispatches timed with the flight schedule.
